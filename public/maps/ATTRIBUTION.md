# 日本の白地図

Original: [Blank map of Japan new.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blank_map_of_Japan_new.svg)
Author: Lincun; modifications by Erida539 and YasutoTakenaka.
License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Game modifications: normalized Yamagata and Fukushima prefecture IDs to 6 and 7; white fill, grey boundaries, green selected prefecture, thin dashed Okinawa inset boundary; accessible Japanese title. The original path geometry is unchanged. Okinawa and southern islands are shown in an inset as in the source. All modified SVG maps in this directory are distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Original and normalized source: `sources/japan-original.svg`, `sources/japan-normalized.svg` in the repository. Reproduce with `npm run maps`.
